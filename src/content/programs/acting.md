Our Acting Classes provide focused training for students who want to develop
their dramatic skills. Whether preparing for auditions or simply wanting to
improve, this program offers professional-level instruction.

## What's Covered

- Script analysis and interpretation
- Character development techniques
- Voice and movement for actors
- Scene study and monologue work
- Audition preparation
