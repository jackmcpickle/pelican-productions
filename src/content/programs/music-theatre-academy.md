The Music Theatre Academy is our comprehensive performing arts program designed
for young performers who want to develop their skills in all areas of musical
theatre.

## What You'll Learn

- Vocal technique and harmonisation
- Dance styles including jazz, contemporary, and musical theatre
- Acting for the stage
- Audition preparation
- Performance skills and stage presence

## Program Structure

Students are placed in groups based on age and experience level. Each term
culminates in a showcase performance for family and friends.
