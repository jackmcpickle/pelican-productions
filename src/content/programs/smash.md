SMASH is our introductory program designed specifically for our youngest
performers. Through games, songs, and creative play, children develop confidence
and a love for the performing arts.

## Program Highlights

- Age-appropriate singing activities
- Creative movement and basic dance
- Drama games and improvisation
- End-of-term mini showcase
