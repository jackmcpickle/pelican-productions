**Bring It On: The Musical** was an electrifying production featuring
high-energy dance numbers, competitive spirit, and a story about overcoming
obstacles.

## Production Highlights

- Original choreography including tumbling and stunts
- Contemporary pop/hip-hop musical score
- Themes of teamwork, integrity, and perseverance

## Cast

Our senior students delivered outstanding performances in this demanding show
that combined singing, acting, and athletic choreography.
