Disney's **The Little Mermaid Jr.** brought the magic of the ocean to the stage
with our talented young performers.

## About the Production

Our junior ensemble brought this classic Disney story to life with colourful
costumes, energetic choreography, and beloved songs including "Under the Sea"
and "Part of Your World."

## Cast & Crew

This production featured students from our Music Theatre Academy and SMASH
programs.
