**In The Heights** tells the universal story of a vibrant community in New
York's Washington Heights neighbourhood - a place where the coffee from the
corner bodega is light and sweet, the windows are always open, and the breeze
carries the rhythm of three generations of music.

## Show Details

- **When:** November 2024
- **Where:** The Nest Studio
- **Duration:** Approximately 2.5 hours including interval

## About the Show

This high-energy musical features a mix of Latin music, hip-hop, and traditional
Broadway styles. Our talented young performers bring this vibrant story to life
with passion and energy.
