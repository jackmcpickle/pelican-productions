Pelican brought Disney's The Little Mermaid JR back in March 2019. Based on the Hans Christian Andersen story, it follows Ariel, a young mermaid who longs to leave her underwater kingdom for the world above.

After she disobeys her father King Triton and explores the human world, Ariel makes a deal with the sea witch Ursula and pays for it. The production was full of colour, energy, and the songs audiences already knew.
