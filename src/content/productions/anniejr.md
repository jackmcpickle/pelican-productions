Based on the comic strip and the Tony-winning musical, with book and score by Thomas Meehan, Charles Strouse and Martin Charnin, Annie JR. puts everyone’s favourite redhead in her first adventure.

With pluck and positivity, little orphan Annie charms 1930s New York City even though she starts with next to nothing. She is determined to find the parents who left her on the doorstep of an orphanage run by the cruel Miss Hannigan. Annie foils Hannigan’s plans and finds a new home with billionaire Oliver Warbucks, his secretary Grace Farrell, and a mutt named Sandy.

The score includes Tomorrow, Hard Knock Life, Maybe and You’re Never Fully Dressed Without a Smile.
