In 2014 Pelican took on the kingdom fathoms below, where Ariel longs to leave her ocean home for the world above. The score includes Part of Your World, She's in Love, and Under the Sea.

The production won the Adelaide Theatre Guide awards for Best Ensemble and Best Technical in the amateur category.
